Lexis AI Corporate Mobility & Remote Work Guidelines
Document Code: HR-POL-01
Last Updated: July 09, 2026

Section 1: International Remote Work Policies
1.1 Duration Limits
Employees are permitted to work remotely from an international location for a maximum of 90 days within a rolling 12-month period. This limit is strictly enforced to prevent triggering permanent establishment risks for the company. Any remote work extension beyond 90 days must be approved by the Executive Committee.

1.2 Employee Tax Compliance and Responsibility
The employee is solely responsible for determining and fulfilling any personal income tax obligations, visa requirements, and social security payments in the host country. Lexis AI will not provide legal or tax advice for individual employee relocations.

1.3 Corporate Tax Implication Guidelines
Working beyond the 90-day threshold requires executive VP approval and a formal review by the Global Tax Office to assess corporate tax liabilities. Permanent establishment issues can create significant corporate tax exposure for Lexis AI in foreign jurisdictions.
